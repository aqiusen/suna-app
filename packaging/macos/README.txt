Suna for macOS

M series Mac, including M1/M2/M3/M4: use darwin-arm64.dmg.
Intel Mac: use darwin-amd64.dmg.

Install:
1. Open the DMG.
2. Drag Suna.app to Applications, or double-click Install Suna.command.
3. Open Suna from Applications.

If macOS says the app cannot be opened, first confirm that the DMG architecture matches your Mac.
